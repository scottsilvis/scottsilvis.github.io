About

I am a Research Associate III in Animal Health Research at Auburn University College of Veterinary Medicine. My scientific background spans bovine virology, diagnostics, vaccine challenge studies, molecular assays, sequencing, experimental design, statistical analysis, and research data management.

Alongside my laboratory work, I am expanding into data science and research informatics. My interests center on using statistics, machine learning, relational databases, and reproducible computational workflows to make complex biological data easier to analyze, interpret, and use for practical decision-making.
